Privacy Policy

Delete your account or data

You can delete your Fluxub account and associated data from the app, or request deletion by email. This section is the deletion guide for the Fluxub app on Google Play.

Delete in the Fluxub app

  1. Open Fluxub and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when asked.

Deleting your account removes your account and associated personal data from Fluxub’s systems used to run the service, subject to the retention notes below.

Request deletion by email

Prefer not to use the in-app flow, or want only some data removed without deleting your whole account? Email support@fluxub.com from the address or phone number on your account.

In your message, say whether you want:

  • Account deletion — delete your account and associated data, or
  • Partial data deletion — delete specific data while keeping your account.

We will confirm the request and complete deletion within a reasonable time (typically within 30 days), unless we must keep certain records longer for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons.

What is deleted vs kept

  • Deleted: account identifiers you used to sign in, profile details (such as name and photo), and personal data tied to your account that is not required to keep for legal or security reasons.
  • May be kept for a limited time: backups, security logs, and records we are legally required to retain; these are removed or anonymised when no longer needed.
  • May remain for others: content you shared in a property context (for example passes or membership history visible to managers/guards) may remain in that property’s records where needed for the property to keep operating, or may be anonymised.

This Privacy Policy explains how Fluxub (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Fluxub mobile app (com.fluxub.main) and related services.

Who we are

Fluxub helps people manage gated properties, access, and related activity on their phones. Questions about privacy: support@fluxub.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information — such as your phone number, name, and profile details you provide when you sign up or set up your person profile.
  • Profile photo — if you choose to take or pick a photo (camera or photo library).
  • Property and access data — information you create or receive in the app about properties, memberships, invitations, and gate access / passes.
  • Location — only when you use “Use Current Address” (or similar) to fill in a property address. We do not continuously track your location in the background for this purpose.
  • Device and diagnostics — technical data such as device type, OS version, crash logs, and performance signals, including through error reporting tools.
  • Usage analytics — aggregated or pseudonymous product usage events to understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • On-device data — some data (including tokens and offline-related data) may be stored securely on your device so the app can work when you are signed in or offline.

How we use information

  • Provide and operate Fluxub (accounts, properties, access, syncing).
  • Show you the right property and activity content for your memberships.
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs (including crash reporting).
  • Understand product usage and improve the experience.
  • Communicate with you about the service when needed (for example support).
  • Protect security and prevent abuse.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us run the app (for example hosting, analytics, and crash reporting), under obligations to protect your data.
  • Other people in your property context when the feature requires it (for example members, managers, or guards related to a property or pass you create or use).
  • Legal / safety when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Fluxub, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can delete your account in the app or ask us to delete account-related data by contacting support@fluxub.com (see Delete your account or data). Signing out on a device removes local session data on that device as designed by the app.

Your choices

  • You can deny camera, photos, or location permission in system settings; some features will not work without them.
  • You can delete your account in Settings, or request access, correction, account deletion, or deletion of specific data by emailing support@fluxub.com.

Children

Fluxub is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, where privacy laws may differ. We take steps appropriate to the service to protect information when it is transferred.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Fluxub after an update means you accept the revised policy.
